# Eiffel Tower

The Eiffel Tower is a wrought-iron lattice tower on the Champ de Mars in Paris, France. It is named after Gustave Eiffel, whose company designed and built the tower.

The tower was constructed from 1887 to 1889 as the centerpiece of the 1889 Exposition Universelle, a world's fair held to celebrate the 100th anniversary of the French Revolution. Many prominent French artists and intellectuals initially criticized its design.

When it was completed, the tower was the tallest man-made structure in the world, a title it held until the Chrysler Building in New York City was finished in 1930. Its height is about 330 metres including antennas. The tower has three levels open to visitors, with restaurants on the first and second levels.
